7. Root Cause Analysis

Breeding sites untouched: Nebulization does NOT deactivate breeding sites or destroy eggs. In the best solution (Elite_1), 720/720 BS (100.0%) are in nebulized blocks but remain active. Eggs continue hatching (rate=0.125/cycle) and mosquitoes in remaining blocks oviposit (rate=0.02/cycle), quickly replenishing the killed mosquitoes.
Nebulization is a one-time event: Mosquitoes are only killed during load_starting_scenario (cycle 0). After that, all 14 cycles run without any intervention. New mosquitoes born from eggs and oviposition are unaffected.
Solution 'Naive' misses top-infection blocks: Only 0/10 of the blocks with highest baseline infections are nebulized by this solution. The optimization objective (deterministic OF from real cases) may not align well with where the simulation generates new infections.
